The project

Slow down, feel, find your centre again

Horses of Florence was born in the heart of the Florentine Chianti from a simple idea: time spent with a horse can become a way back to yourself.

A horse lives in the present and responds to who we truly are, not to who we would like to appear. Beside a horse, we learn to listen again: to our breath, our body, our emotions.

We bring together the beauty of the Florentine countryside, with its vineyards, olive groves and stone villages, and the depth of the bond with the animal. An invitation to pause, to feel and to find your balance again.

The horse is not just a companion, but a guide.

  • Slow down

    Leave the rush behind and find the rhythm of the walk, of your breath, of nature.

  • Feel

    Notice what the horse perceives, and what you perceive through the horse.

  • Find your centre

    Go home lighter, with new energy and a clearer view of yourself.

A welcome made to measure

We welcome no more than one to three riders at a time, so that the instructor can take care of even the smallest details. You choose the pace: we take care of the rest.

On the ground and in the saddle

Two paths, one harmony

We work with the horses both on the ground and in the saddle, with two main aims.

1For you and your horse

Improve the bond with your horse

To get to know your horse more deeply, communicate more clearly and strengthen the bond with your companion in life or at work.

  • Groundwork and ridden work
  • Listening and communication
  • Mutual trust

2For you

Find yourself again

To move past emotional blocks, find yourself again, regenerate and recharge, at the slow pace of the Tuscan countryside.

  • Move past emotional blocks
  • Find yourself again
  • Regenerate and recharge

Choose your experiences

Beyond your time with the horses

The Florentine Chianti invites you to explore. Around the B&B, gravel roads, footpaths and flavours make every stay unique.

  • Fortified villages and castles

    Small stone villages, ancient walls and castles on the hills: the most authentic Tuscany, close at hand.

  • Florence, Siena and beyond

    The cities of art and other destinations, some just half an hour away.

    from 30 minutes

  • By bike or on foot along the Pesa

    From the B&B to Cerbaia in Val di Pesa, along the cycle path that follows the stream. Bikes available to hire.

  • Pool, padel and fishing

    For an afternoon of sport or pure relaxation, near the B&B.

  • Local olive oil and wine

    Olive oil and wine tastings at local producers.

Your stay

A home among the hills

To enjoy the experience without hurrying, you can stay at the B&B A spasso con Daisy · Chianti Home: two apartments in Barberino Tavarnelle, in the heart of the Chianti, a short walk from the Pesa stream.

A holiday, or even just a weekend, to live as a journey towards yourself.
